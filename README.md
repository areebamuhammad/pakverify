# 🇵🇰 PAKVERIFY

### Ruk. Check. Phir Share.

**PAKVERIFY** is an AI-assisted claim verification tool designed for Pakistani digital communities. It helps users pause before forwarding viral messages by extracting claims, checking them against trusted evidence, and providing a simple explanation in Roman Urdu.

---

## 🚨 The Problem

Unverified information spreads rapidly through:

- WhatsApp groups
- Social media
- Community groups
- Family chats
- Viral posts and forwarded messages

The problem is not only fake news.

A bigger problem is that people often **forward information before checking whether there is reliable evidence behind it.**

PAKVERIFY is designed to introduce a simple habit:

> **Ruk → Check → Samjho → Phir Share**

---

## 💡 Our Solution

PAKVERIFY acts as a verification assistant between a user and the information they are about to share.

The user enters a claim or forwarded message.

PAKVERIFY then:

1. Extracts the main claim.
2. Identifies the information that needs verification.
3. Searches its trusted evidence layer.
4. Compares the claim against available evidence.
5. Produces a verification verdict.
6. Explains the result in simple language.
7. Provides Roman Urdu guidance.
8. Tells the user whether they should share, verify further, or avoid forwarding.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🔎 AI-Assisted Claim Extraction

PAKVERIFY identifies the core factual claim from a longer message.

### 🛡️ Evidence-Based Verification

The system uses a curated trusted-source evidence layer rather than pretending that unsupported information is automatically true or false.

### 🟢 VERIFIED

Used when available trusted evidence supports the claim.

### 🟡 UNVERIFIED / INCONCLUSIVE

Used when sufficient supporting evidence cannot be found.

This is important because:

> **No evidence found does not automatically mean a claim is false.**

### 🇵🇰 Pakistan-Focused

The project is designed around information commonly shared in Pakistani digital communities.

### 🗣️ Roman Urdu Explanations

Verification results are presented in simple Roman Urdu so they are easier to understand and act upon.

Example:

> "Is claim ke support mein trusted evidence nahi mila. Bina tasdeeq ke aage forward na karein."
